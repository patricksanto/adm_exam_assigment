The Scenario

User story: As a transport operations manager, I want to understand bus reliability across routes and different times of day, so that I can identify where the service is underperforming and communicate realistic expectations to passengers.

Dataset: Scheduled and actual arrival times for routes 673, 674, and 675 at the 3rd & Pike bus stop, Seattle, March–May 2016.

Two analytical questions:

How reliable are bus routes over time?
What waiting time can passengers expect at different times of day?
